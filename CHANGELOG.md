# Changelog

Notes for the current version. Every earlier version is on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/LeLibrary/LeLibrary/releases).

## [6.0.2]

### Fixes

- The Hub's New Movies row now goes by a film's digital release date instead of its cinema date. A film that goes digital today appears at the top, and films stay in the row for 12 months from their digital release. Films TMDB has no digital date for fall back to their cinema date. New & Returning Shows is unchanged.
- Unlocking a saved setup no longer flashes the password box and bot check back while the settings open. A wrong password still brings the check back for another try.
